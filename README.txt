# Wolfsbane Coffee
	Wolfsbane is a website created for a coffee shop that is created to fill in the vacuum that is the lack of high-quality sites for customers, Wolfsbane is a fully functional website, promoting and demonstrating the advantages and services of the said coffee shop while also helping consumers experience the coffee-house experience from a click of a button 
**Link to project:** https://wolfsbanecoffee.netlify.app/
## How It's Made:

**Tech used:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
This project is a product of love, and unyielding passion to create a service for the customers, by someone that enjoys coffee houses and what they stand for; friendship, conversation, and good times.
During the production phase of this project, a lot of time was spent to find ways to optimize and enrichen the user experience, after countless hours of research and finding out what works out best for the current market of projects that aim at coffee houses, work was begun, taking inspiration of the feel and 'hominess' factor of popular sites and coffee shops, work was aimed at building a website that would feel like home instead of a corporate beige setting, which in my opinion deters many potential long-term customers and regulars.

## Lessons Learned:
During the production of this project, truly important lessons were learned, and the limitations of working alone were painstakingly obvious, yet after spending days on end on finding out the problems and trying to optimize the efficiency of the site, I got hands-on experience of what it is like to work on self-induced deadlines, and trying to meet them, the importance of using technologies like Git, and other things I should've implemented, yet didn't and now with keeping those in mind, I'm excited and thrilled to work on future projects with all those frameworks, and techniques in mind.
